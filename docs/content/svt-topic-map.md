# Student Visa Tax: blog topic map

Jo, 26 Oct: every relevant WHT blog topic moves to SVT, rewritten for students, in English,
Chinese and Vietnamese. Not article by article: by topic. WHT has 142 articles; many are
specific to working holiday visas (417/462 rates, 88 days, farm work, second-year visas) and do
not apply to students. This map decides what SVT covers, in which batch, and what changes.

Rules for every article: facts verified against the ATO / Services Australia / Fair Work and
recorded in `svt-facts-2026-10.md` (or a later facts file) BEFORE writing; residency always
"may be"; never "we are a tax agent"; no refund promises; official sources linked; reviewed in
zh and vi by an independent agent; no immigration advice (visa conditions are Home Affairs'
business, we link to them at most).
