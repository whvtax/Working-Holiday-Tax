# Working Holiday Tax: image prompts for Gemini (draft, Jo to approve)

Rules for every image
- No faces and no hands (AI hands and faces are the giveaway). Objects and places only.
- No readable text, no logos, no real brand names, no amounts of money, no flags, no kangaroos, no beach.
- Must look like a casual documentary phone photo, not a polished render: natural light, a little clutter, wear and dirt on surfaces, slightly off-centre framing, muted real colours.
- Never presented as a real customer, a testimonial or a result. They are scene setting only.
- Generate 4 variants per prompt, pick the most ordinary looking one. Reject anything with a plastic sheen, perfect symmetry, melted text or warped objects.
- Export: WebP, under 120 KB each after compression, fixed width and height in the HTML, loaded below the first screen only (lazy).

Shared style line (paste at the start of every prompt)
"Candid documentary photo taken on a mid-range smartphone, natural available light, slightly imperfect framing, real surface wear and dust, muted natural colours, moderate depth of field, no text, no logos, no people, no faces, no hands."
