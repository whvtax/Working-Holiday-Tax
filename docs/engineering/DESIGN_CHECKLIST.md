# Design quality checklist

Standard: a premium agency site built for this brand and this audience, not a template.

## Before coding a page
- [ ] The page's argument is written as plain text first: one claim per section, each with
      its proof. Sections without a claim are cut.
- [ ] Desktop, tablet and phone layouts are planned (phone first).
- [ ] Uses the SVT design system tokens and components only; any new pattern is added to
      the system, not improvised on the page.

## Visual
- [ ] No generic AI tells: gradient washes, glass cards, rows of icon cards, eyebrow labels
      over every heading, 01/02/03 markers, hairline rules everywhere, everything centred,
      one large radius on everything, emoji icons, invented logos or reviews.
- [ ] One lime highlight per screen at most; lime never as text on white.
- [ ] Type: Be Vietnam Pro for words, IBM Plex Mono for prices and dockets; five sizes at
      most per page; headings fit in two lines on a phone.
- [ ] Real or Gemini-generated images that show atmosphere only, never fake customers,
      staff or reviews.
- [ ] Consistent spacing scale; hierarchy readable at a glance.

## Interaction
- [ ] Touch targets at least 48 px; sticky WhatsApp bar never covers focused content.
- [ ] Motion only where it explains something (the price check); respects reduced motion.
- [ ] Visible focus states; keyboard reachable.

## Content
- [ ] Copy passes the humanizer review (no staged contrasts, triads, filler, AI words).
- [ ] Legal wording exactly as in CLAUDE.md.
- [ ] Chinese and Vietnamese copy written and checked by native speakers.

## Verification
- [ ] Screenshots at 390, 768 and 1440 px reviewed; generic-looking sections reworked.
- [ ] Long strings (Vietnamese) and Chinese line breaks checked.
- [ ] web-design-guidelines and ui-ux-pro-max reviews run; findings fixed.
- [ ] WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and axe checks pass.
