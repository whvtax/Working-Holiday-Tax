# Will audit 3: policy guard and send-path safety checks

Date: 2026-11-17. Read-only audit. No file under src/, the playbook or other docs was changed.

Scope: src/lib/will/policy-guard.ts, reviewer.ts, text-normalize.ts, engine.ts, channel.ts, service.ts, plus the send call sites in scheduler.ts and src/app/api/will/actions/route.ts.

Method: code read with file:line citations. Behaviour was checked by running the real modules in a scratch copy at /tmp/w3 (probe tests in /tmp/w3/__tests__/audit3-probe*.test.ts, outputs in /tmp/w3/probe*-out.txt). Items marked PROBE were observed by running the code.

Notes on the scope: scrubCustomerText and brandLeaks are in src/lib/will/brand-voice.ts (brand-voice.ts:123 and :162), not text-normalize.ts. There are two stripDashes functions: text.ts:29 (used by claude.ts, reviewer.ts, paperwork-checklist.ts and brand-voice.ts) and text-normalize.ts:62 (used by engine.ts). They differ (see F6).

## Answers to items 3 to 6

- Item 3 (reviewer): it runs on any language. Its rule list is English only, with a generic "customer's language" instruction. It passes no zh, vi or pt rule set, so for those languages it depends on the guard, which has no zh or vi rules (F1). It fails open on every error path (F10).
- Item 4 (rule files not enforced in code): the no-bullets rule (F8), the one-emoji rule for money and complaints (F7), the agent-title ban beyond the covered forms (F9), the zh and vi forbidden terms (F1, F9, F13), and the Chinese-for-SVT-only rule for WHT chats (F1). The no-dash rule is enforced on the main path but not on the paths in Table 2 (F5, F6).
- Item 5 (brandLeaks by language): full English names and domains are caught in every language, including pt and vi (probe). Not caught: zh (no translated names), the WHT and SVT abbreviations, and zero-width breaks (F13).
- Item 6 (approved text failing checks): no accidental failures (F19). Two checks fail approved wording by design: PRE_PAYMENT_MEDICARE_SCRIPT before the form (F19), and SALES_CONTENT_AFTER_PAYMENT for paid customers (F16).

Scratch files written outside the repo: /tmp/w3 (copy and probe tests). Nothing in the repo was changed except this report.
