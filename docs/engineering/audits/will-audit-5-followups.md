## Summary

- 16 findings: 1 high, 8 medium, 7 low.
- For scheduled free text, the 24-hour rule is applied in one place: the planner (language-30oct.ts:235, called at scheduler.ts:2008). ## Answers to the other questions

**1. How the 24-hour window is measured.** The clock is customer.lastCustomerMsgAt, the time of the latest inbound row (store-supabase.ts:627-628; store-file.ts:293). It is computed in these places:
- insideServiceWindow, 24 h (language-30oct.ts:184-190): scheduler.ts:2008 (follow-ups) and scheduler.ts:2515 (sweep).
- WINDOW_MS (channel.ts:28) in deliverOut's fallback when a template is missing (channel.ts:594-605).
- A 23-hour margin, REVIEW_THANKS_WINDOW_MS (scheduler.ts:139), for refund check-in, review ask and resend (scheduler.ts:981, 1551, 1627).
- policyGuard, only when isApprovedTemplate is false (policy-guard.ts:1264-1268).
- Manual sends (actions/route.ts:160-162) and approval of queued drafts (actions/route.ts:486-500).
- The inbox banner (Dashboard.tsx:344), and the inbound engine guard, which uses processing time (service.ts:1262-1267).

No free-text follow-up after 24 hours was found by the code's own clock. Text is chosen only when the window is open at fire time (language-30oct.ts:235), and deferred jobs are re-planned at fire time (scheduler.ts:2005-2010). A free-text fallback after a missing template is refused outside the window (channel.ts:594-605). The ways this can still go wrong are F-08 to F-12.

**4. Would verifyTemplates catch a missing template?** Partly.
- Yes: a required English name that is missing or unapproved in any language (channel.ts:942-948, 956; health/route.ts:243-245).
- No: a missing German or Japanese translation of a required key (F-02); the SVT twins (F-01); an unknown approval when the WABA id is unset (F-03).
- Missing de/ja price nudges show only as "optional, sent as text inside 24h" (channel.ts:966). They are not red, and outside the window they skip silently (scheduler.ts:2019).
- The check runs only when WhatsApp is live (health/route.ts:228).

**5. Can a deferred follow-up go outside the window, or stack?** No stacking or out-of-window text was found.
- Each re-run recomputes the window (scheduler.ts:2005-2010), and text is chosen only inside it (language-30oct.ts:235).
- Each deferral cancels its own job before re-adding (scheduler.ts:1864-1865, 1875-1876, 1888-1890, 2012-2013).
- reconcileSchedule cancels FOLLOW_UP and AUTO_CLOSE before re-arming (scheduler.ts:249). scheduleFollowUp itself only adds (scheduler.ts:159-215), so it relies on its callers cancelling first.
- The scheduler header says a single-flight mutex exists (scheduler.ts:4). Not verified.
- Follow-ups go out only 19:00-23:00 Sydney (config.ts:52). deferToMorning moves a job to the next 19:00 (config.ts:213-221).
- Busy defers can run eight times at two hours each before the step is dropped (scheduler.ts:1887-1896), so step timing can drift.

**6. Risk of Meta refusing free text.** Yes, through F-09 to F-12. Refusal is asynchronous: the code's own comment says Meta accepts free text with a 200 and reports error 131047 later through the webhook (channel.ts:583-586). The scheduler therefore records follow_up_sent (scheduler.ts:2137-2139) and advances the cadence (scheduler.ts:2152). A refused nudge is not retried, and the failure surfaces later as a generic card (channel.ts:583-589).
