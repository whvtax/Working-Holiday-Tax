## Policy guard cases (task 7)

Every translation was run through `policyGuard` in the context it is sent in (paid or not, the state, `isApprovedTemplate` true and false, `guaranteeBarred` for ABN and foreign income). A permanent test does the same. Cases found and how they were decided; no guard rule was weakened globally:

1. Japanese and Chinese approved sentences did not match their own corpus entry: the guard folds its input with NFKC (full-width ？（）：＄ become ASCII), the corpus did not. Fix: the corpus and the Library are normalised the same way. Recognition only; no rule changed. This also cleared FORBIDDEN_AMOUNT on "$300" in the ja/zh expenses intake.
2. `BANK_DOCUMENT_MSG.ja` and `OWN_ACCOUNT_MSG.ja` hit ACCOUNT_NAME_WRONG: ご本人名義 ("in your own name") is in the account-name word list. Faithful translation; the rule is about naming our account. Fix: a text that is word for word an approved text is not a wrong account name. Any other text is checked as before.
3. French `REQUEST_ABN_MSG`, `EXPENSES_INTAKE_MSG`, `EXPENSES_DONE_MSG` hit SALES_CONTENT_AFTER_PAYMENT on "frais professionnels" (work expenses; "frais" is also "fees"). Fix: "frais professionnels" is expense talk (EXPENSE_TALK); "frais" alone stays a fee word.
4. FORM_LINK_BEFORE_PAYMENT recognises the payment confirmation by its phrase; the Chinese and Vietnamese phrases were added.
5. The translations are approved wording in the corpus but do not size the length allowance (the French one-message expenses intake is about 2,300 characters and would make the essay rule meaningless).
