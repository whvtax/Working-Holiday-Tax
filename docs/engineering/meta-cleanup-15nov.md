# Meta / WhatsApp clean-up list (15 Nov 2026)

Source: the error counts in the Will export. These are actions for Jo in WhatsApp Manager and
the Meta Business settings. Nothing here changes code.

## Errors seen in the export
| Count | Error | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 98 | 131049, "healthy ecosystem" (quality hold on marketing templates) | Check the account quality rating. Send fewer marketing templates to cold leads until it recovers. |
| 74 | 404, template name does not exist in the translation | Delete or fix the template in WhatsApp Manager, or stop sending it. |
| 28 | 131047, more than 24 hours since the last customer message | Only send templates outside the 24-hour window. Do not send free text. |
| 28 | 131042, WhatsApp Business account billing problem | Confirm the payment method and balance in Meta Business settings. |
| 13 | 131026, number cannot receive WhatsApp | Mark these numbers as unreachable so Will stops trying. |
| 7 | 130497, account restricted from messaging a country | Check the restriction notice in Meta Business settings. |
| 6 and 5 | review_request_en / review_ask_en not in WhatsApp Manager | Create the templates in WhatsApp Manager and get them approved, or leave them off. |

## Win-back templates
The win-back feature was removed from the code (15 Nov). Its templates may still exist in WhatsApp
Manager. Delete them there if they are no longer wanted.
