# Went-cold chats: what the 3-month export shows (15 Nov 2026)

Source: the full Will export, 679 conversations, 16,830 messages. Counts come from a script
over the export (headers and message text), not from reading each chat. Treat them as
directional. No customer names or contact details are copied here.

## Findings
- 224 of 679 chats were auto-closed as "went cold".
- In 195 of them, the last message was from Will. In 16, from Jo or the team. In 13, from the customer.
- 219 of the 224 had at least one follow-up sent before closing.
- Across all 679 chats, 460 had a follow-up sent.
- Paid rate across all chats: about 31% (209 of 679).

## What this means
- Most went-cold chats end after Will's own follow-ups, not after an unanswered question.
  Follow-up timing and wording matter more than the answer text in those chats.
- The unanswered-follow-up cap (M1, three unanswered sends) limits this. It is not yet proven
  to reduce went-cold closures, so the rate should be re-checked after launch.
