# Keyword map: studentvisatax.com.au, English

Audience: international students on 500 and 485 visas searching in English, mainly from India, Nepal, the Philippines, Colombia and Brazil, plus English searchers in Australia.
Domain: https://studentvisatax.com.au (all URLs below are on this domain).

Sources and rules
- GSC: Search Console export, last 3 months to 2026-11-05. The export has queries and pages as separate lists, not query/page pairs, so "current state" uses the page's own average position from the Pages list; where a query position is quoted it is the query's own average.
- SERP observed: Google results checked on 2026-11-05 (see the list at the end). Page one for these topics is held by student portals and agents (insiderguides.com.au, idp.com, switchliving.com.au, pathwaytoaus.com, eduyush.com, austaxai.com.au, aeccglobal.com.au, hrblock.com.au, taxbne.com.au, newlifeinaus.com.au) plus ato.gov.au and community.ato.gov.au.
- Native phrasing: how South Asian, Filipino and Latin American students write these searches in English (short, "for students", "student visa", "in australia", "can I").
- No search volumes are given. Nothing here is a Keyword Planner number.
- What SVT sells: tax returns ($220 TFN jobs only, $385 with ABN work) and DASP help. TFN and ABN are not paid services, so those keywords are won with guides that funnel to the tax return.
- Site wide, the site gets 1,840 impressions and 21 clicks in 3 months. Every position below is early data from a new site.

## 2. Gaps (max 5)

1. TFN hub page. SERP for "tfn for international students" and "how to apply for tfn as international student" rewards one complete guide (eduyush, austaxai). SVT has 14 TFN articles and no guide level page at /tfn. Add /tfn as a guide (GUIDES_EN) that answers what, how to apply free, documents, wait time and the TFN declaration, and links out to the cluster.
2. ABN hub for students. "abn for international students" and "can international students get an abn" have SERP demand (pathwaytoaus, SBS, community.ato.gov.au) and the only guide page, /abn-delivery, is written around Uber Eats. Either add /abn as a guide or widen /abn-delivery with an "ABN for international students" section and an H1 that names ABN plainly.
3. Tax return service intent. "international student tax return australia" is our clearest buyer query and the home page sits at pos 24.8. There is no page whose title and H1 lead with "international student tax return". Extend the home meta title and hero to say "International student tax return in Australia" and add a short answer block with price, what we need and turnaround.
4. "Tax on DASP" answer. The site's largest query (147 impressions) lands at pos 91 on a weak page. Extend /blog/dasp-tax-rate-international-students with an opening table: 35% on the taxed element, 45% untaxed, 65% only for working holiday super, with an example in dollars, and link it from /superannuation.
5. Can I withdraw super on a student visa while still studying. Searchers from India and Nepal ask this (SERP observed: community.ato.gov.au threads, SBS Punjabi on the 2020 early release). No page says plainly "no, not until you leave and your visa ends". Add an H2 to /blog/super-for-casual-student-workers.
