# SVT keyword map: Vietnamese (studentvisatax.com.au/vi)

Market: Vietnamese international students on visa 500 and graduates on visa 485, searching from Australia (most impressions) and from Vietnam after going home.
Data: Search Console, last 3 months to 5 Nov 2026. The site gets 639 query impressions in total, and only 8 queries are in Vietnamese (25 impressions), so most rows rely on SERP checks and native phrasing. Page positions quoted as "page n imp at pos X" come from the Pages tab; with 1 to 5 impressions they are a signal, not a rank. SERP checks run 5 Nov 2026 with Vietnamese queries.
Evidence key: "GSC n" = impressions of that query in 3 months; "GSC EN n" = the English twin of the query (shows the topic has demand on our site); "SERP observed" = wording seen in ranking Vietnamese pages; "native phrasing" = how Vietnamese students say it in speech, Facebook groups and forums.
Base URL for all pages below: https://studentvisatax.com.au

Who we compete with on page one in Vietnamese: ozlinks.au (education agent, "Những điều cần biết về khai thuế cho sinh viên quốc tế tại Úc", "mã số thuế (TFN)"), aztax.com.vn and accgroup.vn (general "thuế thu nhập cá nhân ở Úc", "Thủ tục hoàn thuế ở Úc" which is really about the airport GST refund), duhocinec.com and other study abroad agents, SBS Tiếng Việt podcasts, the ATO Vietnamese PDF "Tax in Australia", nguoiviettaiuc.com forum ("Tiền hưu bổng có rút được khi về VN?"). For ABN, TFN steps and DASP no Vietnamese page ranks well: Google falls back to ATO, super funds (AustralianSuper, Hostplus, ART, AMP), Stripe, Sprintlaw and Uber in English. That is the opening.

Vocabulary notes: students say "hoàn thuế" for both the yearly tax return refund and the airport GST refund (TRS), and "hoàn thuế xuất cảnh" or "rút super" for DASP. Official Vietnamese material (ATO, PALM scheme) calls super "tiền hưu bổng"; students mostly just say "super". TFN is "mã số thuế" or "mã số thuế TFN"; "làm TFN" is the common verb.

## 2. Gaps (max 5)

1. **"tiền hưu bổng" (official term for super)**. Evidence: SERP observed (nguoiviettaiuc thread "Tiền hưu bổng (Superannuation) có rút được khi về VN?", PALM Vietnamese worker guide, ATO Vietnamese material). No Vietnamese page on the site contains "hưu bổng". Action: extend /vi/superannuation: add "tiền hưu bổng (super)" to the meta description, the lead and one FAQ ("Tiền hưu bổng có rút được khi về Việt Nam không?"), and use it once in /vi/blog/dasp-tax-rate-international-students.
2. **"hoàn thuế xuất cảnh" ambiguity**. Evidence: GSC "hướng dẫn hoàn thuế xuất cảnh úc" 3 at pos 48 and "hoàn thuế ở úc" 7 at pos 41; SERP observed (accgroup.vn uses "hoàn thuế ở sân bay" for TRS). Searchers mean either DASP or the airport GST refund. Action: extend /vi/superannuation with a short section "Hoàn thuế xuất cảnh: rút super hay hoàn GST ở sân bay?" linking to /vi/blog/tourist-refund-scheme-international-students, and add the reverse link from the TRS article to /vi/superannuation and /vi.
3. **Getting money into a Vietnamese bank account**. Evidence: native phrasing ("rút super về Việt Nam", "nhận tiền hoàn thuế ở Việt Nam", "khai thuế Úc khi đã về Việt Nam"); GSC EN "australian tax return from overseas" 5. "Việt Nam" appears in no Vietnamese article body. Action: extend /vi/blog/lodge-from-overseas and /vi/blog/dasp-documents-checklist-international-students with a section on receiving the refund or DASP when back in Vietnam (keep or close the Australian account, overseas address with the ATO, fund payment to a foreign account where the fund allows it).
4. **TFN hub page**. Evidence: SERP observed (ozlinks owns "mã số thuế (TFN)" with a single hub page; 13 TFN articles on our side but no guide level page). SVT has no /tfn guide in any language, so TFN links scatter across blog posts. Action: add a guide /vi/tfn (and /tfn, /zh/tfn) titled around "Mã số thuế TFN cho du học sinh: cách xin, thời gian, giấy tờ" that answers in 3 lines and links to the 13 articles in 1.1.
5. **ABN tax return for non delivery work**. Evidence: native phrasing ("khai thuế ABN", "khai thuế cho người làm ABN" for gia sư, dọn dẹp, nail, freelance); the only ABN service page, /vi/abn-delivery, is framed around Uber Eats and DoorDash. Action: extend /vi/abn-delivery H1 and meta to "Khai thuế cho việc làm ABN (giao hàng, dọn dẹp, gia sư, tự do)" and add a short section for non delivery ABN work linking to /vi/blog/abn-business-expenses-international-students.
