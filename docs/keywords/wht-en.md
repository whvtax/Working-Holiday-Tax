# WHT keyword map: English (workingholidaytax.com.au)

Markets: UK, Ireland, Canada, US and English searchers anywhere (Australia carries most English impressions because users search after arriving).
Data: Search Console, last 3 months to 5 Nov 2026 (query positions are site best average for that query). SERP checks run 5 Nov 2026.
Evidence key: "GSC n" = impressions in 3 months; "SERP observed" = wording seen in ranking titles, ATO community and competitor pages; "native phrasing" = how English speaking backpackers phrase it.
Base URL for all pages below: https://workingholidaytax.com.au

Who we compete with on page one: ato.gov.au (always), Taxback.com, Hostelworld, GoAbroad, pvtistes.net (English dossiers), Canstar and super funds (AustralianSuper, Hostplus, ART) for DASP, Flying Solo and Reddit for ABN.

## 2. Gaps (clear demand, no matching page or section)

1. "claim superannuation" / "superannuation claim" / "super claim" (over 1,200 GSC imp, pos 22 to 38). /superannuation targets "claim your super after leaving Australia" but never answers the bare "how do I claim my super" step by step in the first screen. Extend /superannuation with an opening "How to claim your super (DASP) in 4 steps" section using the exact phrase "claim superannuation", plus HowTo style ordered list.
2. "working holiday visa tax return" / "tax refund" cluster (~450 imp, pos 20 to 60) while "working holiday tax" ranks 7.5 on the homepage. /tax-return lacks a refund focused section that matches backpacker phrasing ("claim tax back", "backpacker tax refund", "how much will I get back"). Extend /tax-return with "How much tax will I get back?" and "Backpacker tax refund explained" sections linking to /calculator.
3. "abn tax rate working holiday" / "how much tax on abn" (native phrasing and Flying Solo threads, no page answers it). Add a section to /abn: "How much tax do you pay on an ABN as a working holiday maker" (15% WHM rate on the first $45,000 applies to ABN income too, no PAYG withheld, set money aside).
4. "lost super" / "find lost super" (~400 imp) mostly lands at pos 30 to 77; current page is generic. Extend /blog/how-to-find-lost-superannuation with the backpacker angle (myGov ATO search, multiple funds after farm and hospitality jobs, super held by ATO after you leave) and the phrase "find lost super with tax file number" (GSC 16 + 7).
5. "dasp tax rate" / "super tax working holiday maker" (~420 imp at pos 12 to 24). The 65% explainer exists but no page states the take home number. Extend /blog/dasp-tax-rate-65-percent-explained with a worked table ($5k, $10k, $20k balance to net DASP) and an FAQ "Why is DASP tax so high" (GSC 21 at 8.6).
