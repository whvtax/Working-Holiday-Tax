# WHT keyword map: Spanish (es)

Site: workingholidaytax.com.au, section `/es`. Markets: Spain, Argentina, Chile, Uruguay, Peru, Ecuador, Mexico (all seven are visa 462 countries).
Prepared 5 Nov 2026 from the WHT Search Console export (last 3 months), live SERP checks and native phrasing. No search volumes are invented.

## How to read the evidence

1. **GSC n impressions**: our Search Console export. Important: the export has **zero Spanish language queries** and the whole `/es` section received **1 impression in 3 months** (`/es`, position 2). The seven target countries produced 1,168 impressions site wide (Spain 563, Mexico 199, Argentina 185, Chile 105, Peru 79, Ecuador 22, Uruguay 15), all on English pages and English queries. Where a GSC figure is quoted below it is the English twin query, all countries, and is labelled as such.
2. **SERP observed**: seen in live results for Spanish queries. The Spanish SERP for these services is thin: yomeanimo.com dominates ("Cómo obtener el TFN", "¿Cómo obtener el ABN?", "¿Cómo hacer la devolución de impuestos en Australia?", "Servicio devolución impuestos Australia", "¿Cómo solicitar la Superannuation?"), then taxbne.com.au/es (DASP posts), SBS Español ("Declaración de renta en Australia: guía para principiantes"), a Humanitix "Tax return workshop español", and otherwise English pages (lawpath, OrbitRemit, pvtistes EN, ATO). No People also ask boxes were visible in the tool output, so none are claimed.
3. **native phrasing**: how Spanish and Latin American backpackers write it. They keep the English nouns (TFN, ABN, tax return, super, DASP, payslip) and wrap them in Spanish verbs: "sacar" (Spain and Southern Cone), "tramitar", "obtener", "solicitar", "recuperar", "reclamar".

"Current state" for every `/es` URL is therefore "page exists but weak (0 GSC impressions)" unless stated otherwise. All URLs below are live routes in the repo.

## 2. Gaps (clear demand, no matching page or section)

1. **"declaración de renta Australia"**. SBS Español titles its guide this way and it is the default term in Chile, Peru, Ecuador and Colombia. The phrase appears 0 times in `/es`. Extend https://workingholidaytax.com.au/es/devolucion-impuestos-australia: one sentence in the lede ("el tax return es la declaración de renta australiana") and one FAQ.
2. **Declaring Australian income at home** ("declarar en España los ingresos de Australia", "Hacienda", plus SII Chile, SUNAT Perú, SAT México). Spaniards ask this every renta season; "Hacienda" appears 0 times in `/es`, and the only coverage is one FAQ line ("¿Esto reemplaza la declaración de impuestos de mi país?"). Extend https://workingholidaytax.com.au/es/blog/tax-obligations-after-leaving-australia with a per country section (residence, tax treaty yes or no, what to keep), and link it from that FAQ.
3. **"jubilación / fondo de pensiones" as synonyms for super**. 0 occurrences of "fondo de pensiones" and none of "jubilación" on the service page. Extend the opening of https://workingholidaytax.com.au/es/superannuation ("la super es el fondo de jubilación obligatorio australiano") and add "solicitar" to the title or H1 to match the yomeanimo wording.
4. **"número fiscal / número de impuestos" for TFN**. 0 occurrences of either. Extend the lede of https://workingholidaytax.com.au/es/tfn ("el TFN es tu número fiscal australiano") and use "obtener" alongside "sacar", since Mexico and Peru say "tramitar/obtener" more than "sacar".
5. **"impuestos con ABN" for delivery riders** is split across three guides and the ABN service page has no section that answers "cuánto pago de impuestos con ABN" with a number. Extend https://workingholidaytax.com.au/es/abn with a worked example (income, no withholding, 15 % rate band, what to set aside) and point the delivery guides to it.
