# WHT keyword map: French (France, Belgium, Quebec)

Site: https://workingholidaytax.com.au (all URLs below are on this domain). Prepared 5 Nov 2026 from the Search Console export (last 3 months), live French SERPs and native phrasing. No search volumes are invented.

## Baseline from Search Console

* The whole /fr section recorded 1 impression in 3 months (/fr home, position 9). No /fr subpage appears in the Pages export, which lists pages down to 1 impression, so every French page below currently has no measurable search visibility. Before content work, confirm in URL Inspection that /fr/tfn, /fr/abn, /fr/tax-return, /fr/tax-return-australie-pvt and /fr/superannuation are indexed and that the sitemap lists them.
* The only French language query in the export is "white card australie" (30 impressions, position 29.8), not a service query; its natural owner is /fr/blog/white-card-australia-working-holiday.
* French speaking countries do reach the site, but through English pages and English queries: France 757 impressions (pos 10.6), Canada 1,292 (pos 7.2, mostly anglophone), Switzerland 272 (pos 6.4), Belgium 180 (pos 6.0).
* Where a French keyword has a strong English twin in GSC, the twin is noted as supporting evidence ("EN twin GSC n"). That shows the question is asked, not that the French phrasing has that volume.
* The French SERP for all four services is dominated by pvtistes.net (dossiers and forum), pvt.fr, australie-guidebackpackers.com and oceaniepourleszeros.com. They are content sites, not agents, and they use the words "tax return", "tax back", "TFN", "ABN", "superannuation" and "DASP" untranslated. French users mix English terms with French grammar, so keywords below do the same.

Evidence labels: "GSC n impressions" (our export), "SERP observed" (seen in the French results while researching), "native phrasing" (how French, Belgian or Quebec speakers write it).
