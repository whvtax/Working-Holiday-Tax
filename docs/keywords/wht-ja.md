# Keyword map: WHT, Japanese (ja)

Site: workingholidaytax.com.au/ja (working holiday makers, visas 417 and 462).
Sources: Search Console export of 5 Nov 2026 (last 3 months, web). The export has no query to page join, so "pos" on a keyword is the site average for that query and the landing page is inferred from titles; "page pos" is the page average across all its queries. SERP checks were run on 5 Nov 2026 in Japanese. No search volumes are given anywhere in this file.

Evidence labels: **GSC n** = n impressions in the export; **SERP** = phrasing seen in titles of ranking Japanese pages; **native** = how Japanese WHV makers say it, no data.

Notes that shape the map:
1. Several super guides were merged into the hub by 301 (`/ja/blog/how-to-apply-for-super-back`, `what-is-superannuation`, `what-is-dasp-super-withdrawal` now go to `/ja/superannuation`). Their GSC rows are history, not live pages.
2. `/ja/tax-return` is written under a rule: no prices on the page, and the business never calls itself a 税理士. Keywords containing 税理士, 安い and 費用 must be won within that rule (see Gaps 1).
3. The homepage `/ja` lists TFN, ABN and DASP terms in its metadata keywords. That is harmless for Google but signals mixed intent; the owners below should be the only pages using those terms in title and H1.

## 2. Gaps

1. **タックスリターン 税理士 安い / 費用** (GSC 95 + 34, pos 7.4 and 6.7). No section answers "what does it cost". Extend https://workingholidaytax.com.au/ja/tax-return with an H2 "代行の費用はどう決まるか" that explains the flat fee, that nothing is taken from the refund, and the refund below fee guarantee. This stays inside the page's no price rule. The fixed fees ($220 TFN income, $385 with ABN, incl. GST) are set in CLAUDE.md, so showing them on the JA page is an owner decision, not a content gap.
2. **メディケア免除申請 / タックスリターン メディケア免除 やり方** (GSC 113 + 79 + 17 + more). /ja/medicare uses メディケア税 and レビー, while searchers use 免除申請. Extend https://workingholidaytax.com.au/ja/medicare: put メディケア免除申請 in title and H1 and add a numbered "やり方" section (Medicare Entitlement Statement, where it goes in the return).
3. **オーストラリア タックスリターン いつまで** (GSC 122 + 54 + 48). The dedicated guide gets zero impressions. Extend https://workingholidaytax.com.au/ja/blog/tax-return-deadline-31-october-working-holiday with いつまで in title and the first sentence, plus the 帰国後 and agent lodgement deadline cases.
4. **タックスリターン やり方 / 自分で / myTax** (GSC 16 combined, pos 11 to 15; SERP shows myTax step guides ranking). Extend https://workingholidaytax.com.au/ja/blog/how-to-lodge-tax-return-working-holiday with a myGov and myTax step section in Japanese, and link to /ja/blog/diy-tax-return-vs-tax-agent-working-holiday for the "自分でやるか" decision.
5. **タックスファイルナンバー 取得方法 / 申請** (SERP: ranking titles use the katakana form). Extend https://workingholidaytax.com.au/ja/tfn: add タックスファイルナンバー（TFN） to title and H1.
