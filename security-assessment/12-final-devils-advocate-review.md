## Objections raised and resolved

1. Could item 1 reject real customers? Resolved: every URL the form sends comes from the upload endpoint, which builds `${brandPrefix}tax-form/...` under the public object path. Checked in submit-tax-form.ts (lines 213 to 259) and upload route (lines 134 to 183).
2. Does the lockout change risk locking out staff on IPv6? Resolved: only attackers or the same /64 share a bucket; recovery is the existing timed lockout.
3. Does the renderer change guide output? Resolved: legacy equivalence test over all guide lines, full suite green.
4. Is the CSP flag really off by default? Resolved: reportOnlyHeaders returns `[]` unless the value is exactly `true`; test asserts `1` and unset both give nothing.
5. Does stripping upload error text hurt customers? Resolved: only internal errors are hidden; the customer-fixable messages are kept via the typed error class. Residual: confirm the front end copy for a 500 on the TFN, ABN and super forms.
6. Sandbox CSP and PDFs (item 6): unresolved, staging test required. This is the only open item.
